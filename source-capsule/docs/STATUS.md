# Project status

## Current maturity

CPP Source Capsule 0.1.0a4 is a first-party research prototype.

The repository contains two narrow adapters, one deterministic capsule builder, one verifier, a JSON Schema, synthetic fixtures, deterministic real-tool probe inputs, and 32 automated controls. First-party local runs admitted outputs generated by Inspect AI `0.3.291` and Promptfoo `0.126.0`; no external party has reproduced them.

## Observed in this repository

- Complete Inspect-shaped results produce evidence finding `SUPPORTED`, recorded applicability `CURRENT`, and processing `COMPLETED` for the included profile.
- Partial Inspect-shaped results remain `INCONCLUSIVE`, even when the observed numeric value is high.
- Promptfoo-shaped rows without `gradingResult` remain `NOT_MEASURED`; the current mapping cannot bind those rows to a subject revision, so applicability is `UNKNOWN`.
- A different supplied current subject revision produces current applicability `STALE` without masking the recorded evidence or processing axes.
- No supplied current revision produces `UNKNOWN`, rather than silently presenting an earlier finding as current.
- Source-backed verification reruns the recorded adapter. Rehashed fabricated measurements and fabricated source-binding paths fail their rederivation checks.
- Inspect `eval.model` is compared with the declared subject revision during construction; a mismatch is rejected.
- Inspect completeness requires consistent total, completed, scored, and unscored counts.
- An explicit source error produces evidence finding `NOT_MEASURED` and processing `ERROR`.
- A Promptfoo failed assertion with a numeric score remains a complete measured result; an ungraded row reporting an error is a processing error.
- A mixed Promptfoo population with one graded result and one ungraded processing error preserves `INCONCLUSIVE` evidence and `ERROR` processing as separate axes.
- Mapping-loss records are rederived with the declared selection. They distinguish declared units/selectors, selected source transformations, source fields outside the narrow adapter scope, and unavailable revision binding.
- In the bounded local real-tool probe, both adapters admitted a four-case mean of `0.75`. Inspect applicability was `CURRENT` because `eval.model` matched the profile; Promptfoo applicability remained `UNKNOWN` because its JSONL output exposed no subject revision.
- In the separate three-case agent-claim probe, Inspect and Promptfoo each retained two structured counterexamples as `REFUTED` and an unavailable effect observer as `NOT_MEASURED`.
- The missing-observer case retained different source-processing states: Inspect completed with no score for that sample, while Promptfoo recorded an assertion-processing error. Both mappings retained the evidence finding as `NOT_MEASURED`.
- Changing the supplied current subject revision from the source-bound synthetic v1 identifier to v2 changes current applicability to `STALE` without erasing the recorded finding.
- The committed public fixtures are allowlisted projections of actual tool outputs. They retain every field used by the per-case adapter and omit raw stack traces, local paths, framework stores, and unrelated environment metadata.

These observations are bounded to the included automated tests and same-author local compatibility probes. They do not establish semantic equivalence between tools, an upstream framework defect, or agent safety.

## Not measured

- Compatibility with upstream versions other than Inspect AI `0.3.291` and Promptfoo `0.126.0`, or with broader export configurations.
- Independent implementation or reproduction.
- Usefulness to evaluation maintainers, auditors, civil-society users, or procurement teams.
- Preservation of nonnumeric, distributional, sample-level, or multimodal claims.
- Accessibility or usability of a viewer.
- Whether the static viewer improves a user's interpretation of `REFUTED`, `NOT_MEASURED`, processing failure, or stale evidence.
- Cryptographic issuer identity, transparency, or authority.
- Semantic truth, safety, causal attribution, or production fitness.

## Next evidence gates

1. Ask an external implementer to reproduce or challenge the mapping from the published specification after the candidate is public.
2. Add independently shareable real-world exports whose metadata and rights have been reviewed.
3. Test whether a user can inspect and contest a capsule without a hosted service.
4. Expand revision binding beyond a single source-reported model string without treating provider labels as revisions.
5. Evaluate nonnumeric, distributional, sample-level, and multimodal claims without flattening source-specific unknowns.
